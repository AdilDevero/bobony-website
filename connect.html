Five steps

How to connect

From a fresh install to standing in the city. If something goes wrong, jump to the fixes at the bottom before opening a ticket.

Setup

01

Own GTA V on PC

Steam, Epic or Rockstar launcher — any legitimate copy. Console versions are not supported.

02

Install FiveM

Download it from fivem.net and let it verify your game files. First launch takes a few minutes.

03

Link your Discord

Our servers authenticate through Discord, so your account must be in the community server before you connect.

04

Press F8 and connect

Open the console and paste the connect string below, or use the direct-connect link from Discord.

05

Load the city

First connection downloads the server assets — around 6 GB. Do it once, on a wired connection if you can.

Before your first session

A few things that make the first night smoother:

  • — A working microphone with push-to-talk configured
  • — Your character's name, background and one goal written down
  • — The rules read once, properly
  • — 25 GB of free disk space for assets and cache
  • — Voice activity set to push-to-talk to avoid hot-mic scenes

Troubleshooting

Common problems

"You are not allowlisted"

Your Discord account is not linked to an approved application, or you are using a different account than the one you applied with. Open a ticket with your character name and we will check the record.

Stuck on "Loading resources"

Cancel the download, clear your FiveM cache folder, then reconnect. If it stalls again at the same percentage, your connection is dropping large files — try a wired link or a different DNS.

No voice in game

Check Windows input device, then the in-game voice settings, then whether you are in the correct voice channel. Push-to-talk is the safest setting and is required in some roleplay scenes.

Low FPS or stutter

Lower texture quality before anything else, disable any graphics mod, and make sure the city is on an SSD. Our client is tuned, but a heavily modded GTA install will still drag.

Queue takes forever

Peak hours are 19:00–23:00 CET. Subscribers and staff get priority; otherwise the queue moves as citizens disconnect. The status page shows live capacity.
