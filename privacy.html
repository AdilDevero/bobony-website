Privacy policy

We collect as little as possible, keep it only as long as needed, and never sell it.

What we collect

  • Discord identity — user ID, username and avatar, used to authenticate you and link your allowlist application.
  • Game identifiers — licence, Steam and FiveM identifiers, used to link your characters and enforce sanctions.
  • Application data — the answers, age confirmation and character details you submit.
  • Connection data — IP address, session times and crash logs, used for security, ban evasion checks and debugging.
  • Purchase data — handled by our payment provider; we store the transaction reference, not your card details.

Why we collect it

To run the allowlist process, operate the servers, moderate the community, protect the service from abuse, deliver purchases and meet legal obligations. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who can see it

Only staff with a role that requires it. Application answers are read by administration and support; moderation logs are read by moderators and administrators. Technical partners (server hosting, payment processing, voice relay) receive only what is necessary to provide their service.

How long we keep it

  • Application data: 12 months after a decision, or while you remain a member.
  • Game identifiers and sanction records: for the life of the community, to prevent ban evasion.
  • Connection logs: 90 days, except entries tied to an active investigation.
  • Purchase records: as long as required by tax and accounting law.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of data we are not required to keep. Open a ticket or write to bcsd@contact.com. We answer within 30 days.

Cookies

This website uses no advertising or tracking cookies. If a future version adds analytics, it will be anonymous and disclosed here first.

Children

The services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly hold data for anyone under 18; if we learn that we do, the account is removed and the data deleted.
