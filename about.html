Where we come from

Bobony Roleplay started in 2021 as a small private server for a group of friends who were tired of the same pattern: a promising city, a handful of copied scripts and a community that burned out in three months. We wanted something slower, more deliberate — a place where the story of a character mattered more than their bank balance.

Four seasons later, the concept has not changed, only the scale. What began with forty citizens on a rented box is now a custom framework serving hundreds of concurrent players, with a development team that ships every week.

Every player portrays a citizen whose choices have real consequences for the community. An honest police officer, an ambitious lawyer, a visionary entrepreneur or an underworld figure — your journey is shaped by your encounters and decisions.

Our philosophy

We describe ourselves as semi-hardcore. That means rules exist to protect stories, not to win arguments. A few principles guide every decision we make:

  • Story over stats. Progression is a tool for roleplay, never the point of it.
  • Consequences are content. Getting caught, losing a case, serving time — these are scenes, not failures.
  • Consistency beats intensity. We would rather have a quiet, believable evening than a chaotic one.
  • Fairness is non-negotiable. Clear rules applied equally to everyone.
  • No pay-to-win. Supporters fund the server; they never buy power.
Our goal is simple: provide a setting where quality roleplay is valued, improvisation meets consistency and everyone can find their place.

The staff team

Staff are players first. Every member of the team started with an allowlist application, and most still run characters in the city. We separate the roles deliberately so no single person controls both the story and the punishment:

  • Administration — server direction, whitelist decisions and policy.
  • Moderation — reports, voice logs, rule enforcement.
  • Support — tickets, technical help and onboarding for new citizens.
  • Development — framework, scripts, UI and performance work.
  • Faction leads — LSPD, EMS, DOJ and business oversight, played in character.

Reports are handled through Discord tickets with a written trail. Every sanction can be appealed, and Internal Affairs reviews police conduct independently.

Built in-house

We do not resell a framework. Our stack is custom from the database up: a tuned persistence layer, a shared reputation and XP engine, and one design language across every interface — inventory, HUD, MDT, radial menu, chat and phone. That is why a full server still holds a steady frame rate during a 200-player event.

Updates ship on a published changelog, and the community votes on the next block of work through polls. You can read the current notes on our news page.

The community

Our community is Moroccan at heart and international by nature. Arabic, French and English are all spoken in character and in support, and new players are paired with a mentor for their first week.

Roleplay quality is protected by a whitelist, but the culture is protected by the people. Respect is the golden rule, toxicity is not tolerated, and the staff team reads every piece of feedback that arrives.
