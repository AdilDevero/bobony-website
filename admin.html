Permissions

Who can do what

Owner

Full permission

Everything below, plus adding, changing and removing staff — and full access to the activity log. Your Discord account always keeps owner access as a backup.

Admin

Review & manage

Review every application, accept or decline it, delete test or duplicate records, and see the staff list and activity feed.

Helper RP

Review & decide

Read applications, accept, decline or reset them, and follow the activity feed. Cannot delete records or change staff.
