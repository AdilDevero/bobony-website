Refund policy

Support goes straight into hosting and development, so we handle refunds carefully and in writing.

Subscriptions

  • You can cancel at any time in the Discord store; access continues until the end of the paid period.
  • Cancelling does not automatically refund the current period.
  • If a subscription was charged in error, or a perk was not delivered at all, we refund the affected period in full.

Cosmetic packs

  • Refundable within 14 days of purchase if the item was not delivered, or was delivered incorrectly.
  • Once a pack has been worn, used or transferred in game, it is no longer refundable.
  • If a cosmetic is removed from the server, affected buyers receive either a replacement or a pro-rata refund.

Bans

If an account is permanently banned for breaking the rules — cheating, harassment, doxxing, hate speech or ban evasion — outstanding purchases are not refunded. This is stated on the terms of service before purchase.

How to request a refund

Open a purchase ticket in Discord with your receipt reference and the character name the item was delivered to. We answer within 72 hours and process approved refunds to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Chargebacks

Please talk to us before contacting your bank. A chargeback filed without contacting support results in suspension of the account and its perks until the dispute is resolved.
