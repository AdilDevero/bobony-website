Frequently asked
questions

Everything new citizens ask in their first week. If your question is not here, open a ticket in Discord — someone answers every day.

What is Bobony Roleplay?

Bobony Roleplay is a premium FiveM roleplay server featuring a fully custom framework, a dedicated development team and a passionate community. We focus on serious roleplay with high-quality scripts and strong performance.

How do I join the server?

Apply for the allowlist through our application page. Connect your Discord, ensure you meet the requirements and submit your application. Alternatively, a trusted player can sponsor you through the Damana system.

What are subscriptions for?

Subscriptions help support the server's hosting and continuous development costs. In return, supporters receive aesthetic perks, queue priority and exclusive Discord roles — without breaking our strict no-pay-to-win policy.

Do I need prior RP experience?

While prior experience is helpful, we welcome newcomers who are eager to learn. We expect all players to read the rules and stay in character at all times.

How do whitelist jobs work?

Whitelist jobs such as Police (LSPD), Medical (EMS) and Mechanics require a separate application in our Discord. Once accepted, you undergo training to ensure high-quality roleplay in these vital community roles.

What are the PC requirements?

A stable copy of GTA V on PC, at least 16 GB of RAM, an SSD with 25 GB free for the city files, and a microphone. A wired connection is strongly recommended — voice roleplay punishes packet loss.

How long does the allowlist take?

Most applications are reviewed within 48 hours. During the first week of a new season it can take up to five days, because we read every answer instead of scanning them.

Is there a queue?

During peak hours the server fills and a queue forms. Subscribers and active staff get queue priority; everyone else enters in order. The status page shows live capacity.

What is the Damana system?

Damana is our sponsorship programme. A trusted citizen vouches for a newcomer and becomes their mentor for the first two weeks. It moves an application into a faster queue — it does not skip the rules or the review.

Can I transfer money or assets between characters?

No. Every character starts clean, and assets stay with the character that earned them. This keeps the economy honest for players who have been here since season one.

What happens if my character dies permanently?

You create a new character with a new background, and the city remembers the old one — businesses, debts and relationships included. Our staff will help you plan a believable entrance.

How do I report a player or a bug?

Use the report system in game for anything live, or open a ticket in Discord with a clip if you have one. Bug reports are rewarded with in-game compensation when they lead to a fix.

Do you accept content creators?

Yes. Streamers and video creators get a verified role, a media channel and priority review. Request it in a ticket with a link to your channel.

Is the server pay-to-win?

No. Subscriptions are cosmetic, convenience and queue priority only. Nothing sold on the store affects combat, economy balance or roleplay outcomes.

Still stuck?

Open a ticket in Discord. Average first reply: under two hours.
