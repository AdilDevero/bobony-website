Damana system

Our sponsorship programme for new citizens.

A trusted citizen can vouch for a newcomer. A sponsorship does not skip the rules or the review — once staff approve it, your friend's dossier joins the normal queue (story review, PC check, then a vocal interview).

As the sponsor you are responsible for their conduct for 30 days. Sponsoring requires at least 20 hours of server playtime.
